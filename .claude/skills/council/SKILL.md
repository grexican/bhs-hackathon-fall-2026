# /council: five advisors, one recommendation

Say the decision in one line and its 2–3 options. Unclear? Ask one question first.

Then each advisor gets **one short paragraph** (3 sentences max), in their own voice:

1. **The user**: someone who'd really use it. What makes them open it again tomorrow?
2. **The skeptic**: what goes wrong? What's assumed? What's the weakest part?
3. **The builder**: what's quickest to build well this weekend, and what's a trap?
4. **The designer**: what will people feel and remember? What's confusing?
5. **The business**: who would pay for it or share it, and does this choice help?

**Synthesis** (4 lines max): where they agree, the real trade-off, and your pick marked
**(recommended)**. Then ask the student to choose: it's their call. Log it in `docs/decisions.md`.
